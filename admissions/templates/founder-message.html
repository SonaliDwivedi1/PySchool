Founder’s Message

Premlata Kumari

Welcome to Einstein Public School, Janakpur.

At Einstein Public School, we believe that education is not simply about academic achievement. It is about nurturing confident, responsible and compassionate young individuals who are prepared to make a positive contribution to society.

Our vision is to provide every child with a supportive and inspiring environment where curiosity, creativity, discipline and strong values can flourish.

We are committed to providing quality education while helping our students develop the knowledge, character and skills they need to succeed in an ever-changing world.

I warmly welcome you to our school community and look forward to seeing our students grow, learn and achieve their dreams.

Premlata Kumari
Founder & Director
