Admissions

Begin your child's journey with Einstein Public School.

Join Einstein Public School

We welcome families who are looking for a supportive, engaging and inspiring learning environment for their children.

Our admission process is designed to be simple and straightforward. Follow the steps below or contact the school if you need more information.

Admission Process

1

Enquiry

Contact the school to learn about available classes, admission requirements and important information.

2

Application

Complete the admission application form and provide the required information and documents.

3

Assessment

Depending on the class, students may be asked to participate in an assessment or interaction.

4

Confirmation

Successful applicants will receive admission confirmation and further instructions from the school.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed admission application form.
  • Student's birth certificate.
  • Previous school records, where applicable.
  • Recent passport-size photographs.
  • Parent or guardian identification documents.
  • Any additional documents requested by the school.

Please contact the school for the exact requirements for your child's class.
